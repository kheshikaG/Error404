SplitSmart AI — Primary Introduction, Overview & Documentation

 1. Introduction
SplitSmart AI is an AI-powered group expense management platform designed to solve a common problem: **shared expenses become difficult to track when multiple people pay, consume different items, and settle at different times.**

Instead of simply dividing a bill equally, SplitSmart AI combines **AI-powered expense understanding, evidence verification, user confirmation, approval rules, deterministic calculations, and payment-status tracking** into one system.

Users can create groups, invite any number of members, add expenses through receipts, typing, or voice, review AI-generated expense information, assign items to participants, and see exactly who owes whom.

The key principle is:

> AI understands the expense. The system calculates the money. People confirm what is true.**

This distinction is important because AI is used to interpret information, but the final financial calculations are performed deterministically rather than generated by AI.

2. Overview

SplitSmart AI is built around four main stages:

Capture → Understand → Verify → Settle

Capture
Users can provide expense information through:

* Receipt images
* Manual entry
* Natural-language text
* Voice
* Payment evidence

Understand
AI processes the provided information and identifies relevant details such as:

* Expense description
* Date
* Amount
* Currency
* Merchant
* Individual receipt items
* Categories
* Potential inconsistencies

The user can review and correct the AI's interpretation before the expense is finalized.

Verify
SplitSmart AI determines how much trust can be placed in an expense based on the available evidence.

For example:

* A receipt can provide supporting evidence.
* A manually entered expense can require group approval.
* A payment proof can support a settlement.
* A cash payment can require confirmation from the recipient.

This creates a human-in-the-loop trust system rather than allowing AI to make financial decisions independently.

Settle
Once expenses are confirmed, SplitSmart AI calculates:

* How much each person paid
* How much each person owes
* Who should receive money
* How outstanding balances can be simplified

Users can then record or verify settlement through the supported payment method.



3. The Problem
Traditional group expense splitting often relies on:

* Manually entering every expense
* Calculators or spreadsheets
* Remembering who paid
* Guessing who consumed which items
* Sending screenshots as proof
* Manually calculating reimbursements
* Repeatedly asking people whether they have paid

This becomes increasingly complicated as the number of people and transactions grows.

The problem is not only "How much does everyone owe?"

It is also:

> "Can everyone understand why they owe it, and is there enough evidence or agreement to trust the amount?"

SplitSmart AI addresses both the **calculation problem** and the **trust/verification problem**.

5. AI Capabilities

SplitSmart AI uses AI where it provides the most value: **understanding unstructured information.

Receipt intelligence
The system can analyse a receipt and extract:

* Merchant
* Date
* Currency
* Total
* Tax
* Individual items
* Quantities
* Prices
* Categories

Users can review and correct extracted information.

Natural-language expenses

Instead of filling a long form, users can describe an expense naturally.
The AI converts the description into structured information that the user can review before confirmation.

Voice expenses
The user can speak an expense.

The process becomes:
Voice → Speech-to-text → AI expense parser → Structured expense → User review

AI explanations
The system can explain how information was interpreted and highlight information that requires user attention.

AI limitations
AI does **not** determine the final financial balance.

The AI interprets information; the backend performs the actual financial calculations.



6. Flexible Group System

A group is not restricted to four people.
A group can contain any number of members supported by the application's infrastructure.

Members can:

* Join through an invitation
* Be added to the group
* View relevant expenses
* Approve expenses where required
* Participate in individual expense items
* Confirm settlements

Different expenses can involve different subsets of the group.

For example, an expense may involve everyone in the group, while another may involve only a few participants.


7. Smart Expense Splitting

SplitSmart AI supports item-level expense allocation.

Instead of:

> Total bill ÷ everyone

the application can determine:

> Which person is responsible for which items?

For each receipt item, users can select the relevant participants.

The system then calculates each person's share.

The core calculation is deterministic:

Item share = Item amount ÷ Number of selected participants

The system can then aggregate the person's shares across all expenses.



8. Trust and Approval System

One of SplitSmart AI's distinguishing concepts is that not every expense should automatically have the same level of trust.

The system considers the available evidence.

Evidence-supported expense
A receipt provides supporting evidence for the amount and items.

Manual expense
A manually entered expense can require approval according to the group's configured approval rule.

Payment evidence
A payment proof can be analysed and compared with the expected settlement.

Cash/manual settlement
A cash payment can remain pending until the recipient confirms receiving it.

This creates a transparent state rather than simply changing an expense to "paid" because someone pressed a button.


9. Deterministic Balance Engine

The balance engine is separate from the AI.

For each confirmed expense, the system determines:

Amount paid by a person − Amount owed by that person = Net balance

A positive balance means the person is owed money.

A negative balance means the person owes money.

The system then simplifies outstanding debts so that unnecessary transactions can be avoided.

For example, instead of requiring several people to pay each other separately, the engine can determine a smaller set of settlement relationships.



10. Settlement System

SplitSmart AI is primarily a **calculation and trust layer**, not a bank.

When a user wants to settle a balance, the system can provide a settlement workflow.

Payment proof
The user makes the actual payment through the supported external payment method and provides proof.
The application can then verify information against the expected settlement.

Manual/cash payment
The payer records that the payment was made.
The settlement remains pending until the recipient confirms receipt.
Only then does the system mark the settlement as confirmed and update the relevant balances.
This prevents the application from treating an unverified payment as completed.


11. Wallet Concept

If a future version introduces an integrated wallet, the wallet should remain conceptually separate from the **expense ledger**.

The expense ledger answers:

> Who owes whom?

The wallet answers:

> How much money is available within the wallet system?

A wallet integration would therefore be a payment infrastructure layer rather than the core calculation engine of SplitSmart AI.

15. What Makes SplitSmart AI Different
Ordinary expense splitters primarily focus on calculating shares. Existing expense-splitting products commonly provide features such as group expenses, receipt scanning, flexible splitting, real-time balances and settlement tracking. ([GitHub][1])

SplitSmart AI's intended differentiation is the combination of:

AI understanding
Receipts, text and voice can be converted into structured expenses.

Evidence-based trust
The system distinguishes between expenses supported by evidence and those requiring approval.

Human-in-the-loop confirmation
Users can review and correct AI interpretations.

Deterministic financial logic
AI does not decide the final arithmetic.

Explainable balances
Users can understand why a person owes or is owed a particular amount.

Verified settlement
A payment is not automatically considered complete simply because someone marks it as paid.

One unifind workflow
Receipt, manual, text, voice and payment evidence ultimately connect to the same expense and balance system.

16. Core Product Philosophy
SplitSmart AI is built around three principles:

Understand
Use AI to understand messy real-world expense information.

Trust
Use evidence, approval and confirmation to establish whether information should be accepted.

Calculate
Use deterministic financial logic to produce accurate balances.

In one sentence:
SplitSmart AI turns messy shared spending into transparent, verified and explainable financial relationships.
